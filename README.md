# The website is live at https://waypointcity.vercel.app
🎉 Waypoint City – Tonight, Sorted.
AI plans it. IoT makes it real. You just show up.
Waypoint City is the AI-powered social OS + IoT wearable platform that takes you from group chat hell → actual nights (or days) that happen. Whether you’re flying solo, discovering new crews, or wrangling flaky friends, Waypoint sorts the vibe in seconds — and then makes it come alive IRL with smart wearables.
✨ What It Does
AI Concierge: Chat like ChatGPT, but instead of essays it builds real itineraries: rooftop cocktails, coastal hikes, thrift crawls, study sessions, techno nights.
Discover Crews: AI matches you into groups of 4–8 based on location, vibe, and timing. Perfect for blind dates, new friends, or just not going out alone.
Friends Mode: Herd your squad into alignment with polls, calendar sync, and automated scheduling. Group project management… for fun.
Waypoint Bands (IoT): The hardware flex. Smart wearables with BLE + NFC + RGB LEDs + vibration motors (+ optional OLED screen). Bands light up your crew color, tap-to-enter venues, buzz for notifications, and sync with DJs for room-wide moments.
Safety First: Public venues only, ID checks, live check-ins, and SOS baked in.
Waypoint Uni (Coming Soon): Meet people at your uni, sync timetables, and plan society events.
🔌 IoT Magic (Why This Isn’t Just Another App)
BLE (Bluetooth Low Energy) → Bands connect to the app or venue gateways in real time.
NFC Tap-in → Bands act as access tokens; tap at doors to auto-check-in.
Live Venue Insights → Gate sensors + band pings = live crowd % and wait times in the app.
Crew Colors → RGB LEDs glow your team color; instant group identity in packed spaces.
Interactive Icebreakers → Bands buzz or display dares/prompts; venues can trigger room-wide games.
DJ Sync → Bands pulse in sync with music, lighting up dance floors.
Firmware & Security → Signed DFU updates, encrypted BLE, rotating NFC tokens.
Battery Life → 8–12 hours of party-proof runtime; recharge between adventures.

🛠️ Tech Stack
Frontend: Next.js 14, Tailwind, shadcn/ui (we abused it to make it sexy).
Backend: Supabase + AI APIs for matching, planning, and smart scheduling.
AI Layer: Chat-style interface, natural language → plans, itineraries, calendar sync.
IoT Hardware: nRF52 / ESP32 MCU + BLE, NXP NFC chips, RGB LED array, haptics, Li-ion battery, DFU OTA pipeline.
